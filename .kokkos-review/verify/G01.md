### F-G01-2

### F-G01-4
VERDICT: CONFIRMED (low; error path only)
reason: At collide_vss_kokkos.cpp:1004 (and likewise 1435, 2161, 2711, 3383) a zero-volume cell only sets d_error_flag and keeps going. attempt_collision_kokkos divides by volume (3766) -> inf, or NaN when vremax is 0. static_cast<int>(inf/NaN) at 1015 is UB. Reaching it requires a zero-volume cell holding >=2 particles, which the CPU itself treats as possible by guarding it.
