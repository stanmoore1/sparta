### F-G09-2
VERDICT: PLAUSIBLE (CPU physics question, not a Kokkos defect)
reason: KK surf_collide_cll_kokkos.h:384-387 is a faithful copy of src/surf_collide_cll.cpp:390-393, so there is no parity issue. The units look suspect: exp(-boltz*vibtemp) is applied to an energy (J) instead of the dimensionless vibtemp/T. The reviewer's "negligible" is wrong, though. For small x, -log(1-R*x) ~= R*x with x = k*Theta_v, so evib_star ~= R*k*Theta_v, numerically a uniform fraction of one vibrational quantum in J. That is the same order as evib and not negligible. Whether the intended formula is -k*Theta_v*log(1-R(1-exp(-Theta_v/T_surf))) or something else is an upstream physics call.
cpu-also: yes
fix: none in KOKKOS; any change must be made in src/surf_collide_cll.cpp first and then mirrored. Recommend no change in this pass.
